Resident Stories

Hear from the people who call Sample Assisted Living their home. These are real stories from our residents about their experiences, friendships, and daily joys.

Margaret Thompson

Age 82

“I've found a second family here. Every day brings something to look forward to.”

Margaret joined us two years ago and has become the heart of our book club. She loves gardening and can often be found tending to our raised beds, sharing her knowledge with other residents and staff.

Favorite Activities:

ReadingGardeningKnitting

Robert Chen

Age 78

“The care here is exceptional, but it's the friendships that make it home.”

A retired music teacher, Robert brings joy to our home with his piano playing. He leads our weekly sing-alongs and has taught several residents to play simple melodies.

Favorite Activities:

PianoChessCooking

Dorothy Williams

Age 85

“I was worried about losing my independence, but here I've gained so much more.”

Dorothy is our resident artist and leads our weekly painting classes. Her watercolors decorate the common areas, and she's always eager to help others discover their creative side.

Favorite Activities:

PaintingBakingBird Watching

Every resident has a unique story. We'd love to share more with you during your visit.
